## Entry criteria
- The card has a title and a description of the idea.

## Work
Keep this fast. One short pass, no rabbit holes.
- Read the idea. If anything about the goal is genuinely unclear, note the ONE question that
  matters — don't invent a list.
- Glance at the repo only enough to confirm the idea makes sense and isn't already built.

## Exit criteria
- You can state, in one sentence, what we are building and why.

## Output
- `IDEA.md` in the card folder — three lines, no more:
  - **What:** one sentence.
  - **Why:** one sentence.
  - **Open question:** the single thing worth asking the human, or "none".
